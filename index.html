The marina

Moorings

Strensham Mill sits on the River Avon in the Worcestershire countryside, close enough to the M5 and M50 to reach easily and far enough from them to hear nothing but the water.

The pontoons are anti-slip, with high-rise poles and mooring rings. We have kept moorings here for more than thirty years. Some of the boats we sell come with the option of a mooring alongside.

  • Anti-slip pontoon platforms, high-rise poles and mooring rings
  • Water and electricity to each pontoon
  • Private, gated and quiet, well off the main navigation
  • Hard standing and level ground for parking

Finding us

Getting here

Mill Lane runs down to the river from the village. The marina is at the end of it.

Directions in Google Maps Apple Maps

  • By road

    Leave the M5 at junction 8, where the M50 joins. Follow signs for Strensham village, then turn into Mill Lane and carry on to the end. Satnav: WR8 9LB.

  • By water

    We are on the Lower Avon Navigation, on the stretch between Tewkesbury and Pershore, a little upstream of Strensham Lock. Call ahead before arriving by boat.

  • Nearby

    Tewkesbury is about fifteen minutes by road and Upton-upon-Severn about the same. Worcester is roughly half an hour, Stratford-upon-Avon about an hour.

Questions

Do you have moorings available?
Availability changes through the year. The quickest way to find out is to ring — we will tell you plainly whether we have space.
Do the boats come with a mooring?
Some do. A mooring at Strensham Mill can be available alongside certain boats. Ask when you call.
Where exactly are you?
Mill Lane, Strensham, Worcester WR8 9LB — on the River Avon, about three minutes from junction 8 of the M5.
Can I visit and look round?
Please ring first rather than arriving unannounced. It is a working marina and a private site, and we would rather meet you properly.
Can I arrive by boat?
Yes, we are on the Lower Avon Navigation between Tewkesbury and Pershore, a little upstream of Strensham Lock. Call ahead.
